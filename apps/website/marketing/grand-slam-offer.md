## 5. Why it's "too good to be true" and still profitable

- Cost of the bonuses is mostly founder time: 100 x 20 min = 33 hours, spread over 4 weeks. Bonus 2 already exists in the product. Bonus 3 costs nothing until we raise the price.
- The outcome guarantee costs at most one refund of $39 / $348 per unhappy buyer. With a 7-day trial we have 0 paid; any lift beats that.
- Setup calls are the best product research we can buy: 100 founders telling us what they automate.
- Founders who are set up with 3 running automations churn far less (Hormozi's "virtuous cycle": commitment, results, retention).
