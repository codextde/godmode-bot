# Positioning (since 2026-10-18)

Godmode is the AI coworker for everyone who works at a computer. You tell it the task in plain words, it does the
computer work start to finish, in your own browser and apps, and hands it back done.

It replaces "Like Grok Bot. But with Claude." (2026-10-07). Grok Bot lives inside Cursor, a code editor, so that line
spoke to developers. The new audience is everybody else.

## Who it is for

Office staff, assistants, freelancers, small-business owners, shop owners, marketers, salespeople, bookkeepers,
recruiters, agencies. People who lose hours a week to logging in, copying, pasting, downloading, filling forms and
writing the same emails. Not developers (they are welcome, but we don't sell to them).

## The promise

- Headline: **Your computer work. Done for you.**
- One line: Tell Godmode what you need. It opens your browser and apps, signs in, clicks through and finishes the job.
- Proof, in plain words:
  - Signs in with your own logins and 2FA codes, and never sees your passwords.
  - Works in the websites and programs you already use. No coding, no IT project.
  - Does the whole job, not a draft: download, fill in, file, send. By default it asks you before important steps
    (sending, paying, deleting, posting) unless the task already told it to go ahead.
  - Runs on its own: every Monday, when an email arrives, when a price changes.
  - Remembers how you like things.
  - Message it from your phone, Slack, Telegram or Teams.
  - Runs on your own Mac.

## Jobs to show (use these, in this order of priority)

1. Email and admin: inbox sorted and replies drafted, meetings booked, forms filled, documents filed.
2. Marketing and ads: ad reports from Meta, Google and TikTok, pausing ads that don't sell, new ad texts, social posts
   scheduled, review replies, competitor prices watched.
3. Finance: invoices collected from portals, receipts matched, payment reminders drafted, spreadsheets updated.
4. Online shop: orders, "where is my order" emails, stock checks, product texts, returns.
5. Sales: CRM updated after calls, leads researched, follow-ups drafted.
6. Research: compare tools or suppliers, market lists, price alerts.

Never lead with coding, bug fixing, pull requests, test suites or servers.

## Claims to keep accurate

- Not "no API keys" or "nothing to connect": instant email/Slack triggers need a connected account (Composio),
  CAPTCHA solving needs the user's 2Captcha/CapSolver/Anti-Captcha key, Teams needs an Azure bot. Say "no coding".
- Not "always asks first": asking is the default, it skips it when the task already says to do that step.
- Passwords are hidden from the AI and blacked out in chats and logs, not in screenshots.
- Banks that confirm logins in their phone app still need the user; say "it asks you when a site wants your phone".
- The always-on version for a spare computer installs with one copy-paste command (we help teams set it up).

## Words

Use: computer work, busywork, the task, start to finish, done, your logins, your apps, plain words, no coding, asks
before anything important, its own Mac.

Avoid in headlines, ads and posts: Claude Code, MCP, API, VM, virtual machine, git, Docker, terminal, headless, CLI,
computer use, browser use, open source, MIT, model names as the hook, "agentic". "Powered by Claude" is fine as a small
trust line. "Agents" is fine once the reader knows what Godmode is ("AI coworkers" first).
